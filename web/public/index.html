Everything you need, nothing you don’t

A small tray app, a small Android app, and a gateway that links them.

Wireless by default

Connect over Wi-Fi or mobile data through your gateway. No cable once it is set up.

Token pairing

Scan the desktop QR or enter its token. A saved session reconnects on its own.

Works with your tools

Use adb, Android Studio or scripts through a local endpoint on 127.0.0.1:44755.

Your gateway

Point Pairspan at your own WebSocket gateway and, if you want, protect it with a shared token.

Up and running in three steps

Install on both devices, open them, done.

Install the desktop appIt lives in your menu bar or system tray and keeps the connection alive.
Install the Android appAllow the permissions it asks for. Pairspan can use ADB’s shell access once wireless debugging is on.
PairScan the desktop QR or enter the token. When the phone shows “Connection ready”, your tools can use it.

macOS says it can’t verify Pairspan?

Pairspan is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time. It is safe to open if you downloaded it from this page or our GitHub releases.

  1. Drag Pairspan to Applications and open it once.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Pairspan.
  3. Or run this in Terminal, then open the app again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Pairspan.app

Opening the APK on your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser. All releases and older versions
