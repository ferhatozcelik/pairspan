Three steps. Done forever.

Set up once with a USB cable, then go wireless permanently.

01 · USB

Plug in via USB once

Open Pairspan on your computer and connect your phone. If the Android app isn’t installed yet, Pairspan installs it from GitHub automatically.

02 · WIRELESS

Unplug & stay connected

Unplug the cable. Even if your phone sleeps or Wi-Fi drops, Pairspan reconnects in the background with a single clean device identity.

03 · YOUR CONTROL

Unpair anytime

The connection stays alive until you remove the phone from your PC app or remove the PC from your phone app.

macOS says it can’t verify Pairspan?

Pairspan is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time.

  1. Drag Pairspan to Applications and open it once.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Or run this in Terminal:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Pairspan.app
